The way you wash matters almost as much as what you wash with. These simple habits help you get the most from your shampoo.

## 1. Wet your hair thoroughly

Make sure your hair is fully wet before applying shampoo. Lukewarm water works well for most people.

## 2. Use the right amount

A coin-sized amount is usually enough for short to medium hair. Use a little more for longer or thicker hair.

## 3. Focus on the scalp

Massage the shampoo gently into your scalp with your fingertips, not your nails. The lather will clean the lengths as you rinse.

## 4. Rinse well

Rinse until the water runs clear. Leftover product can leave hair feeling heavy.

## 5. Be gentle when drying

Pat your hair with a soft towel instead of rubbing, and let it air-dry when you can.

## 6. Find your rhythm

How often you wash depends on your hair type and lifestyle. Pay attention to how your hair feels and adjust your routine to suit you.
